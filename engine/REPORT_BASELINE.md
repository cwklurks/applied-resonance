- NOTE: val acc (0.500) is below the expected 0.85 while train acc is near-perfect. This is an id-disjoint generalization gap, not an eval bug: with only 3 training ids per machine on this 400-clip subset, the CNN latches onto id-specific spectral signatures that do not transfer to the held-out id_06 hardware (the fan/pump log-mel mean ordering even inverts on id_06), so it collapses toward predicting one class. Expect this to recover on the full dataset (more ids and clips); the anomaly scorers above are unaffected since they are fit per-id.
