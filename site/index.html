01 How it works

Learn healthy.
Watch for change.

Each pilot box is assigned to one machine. It learns that machine’s normal pattern locally, without requiring a generic fault classifier to guess what hardware it is hearing.

01

Learn

A microphone and contact accelerometer record a healthy baseline for one pump, fan, or compressor.

02

Detect change

Local scoring compares each new window with that machine’s baseline. Sound and vibration remain separate evidence channels.

bearing_wearimbalancecompressor_knock

03

Alert and report

The pilot path sends a concise alert when the state changes and a plain-language health report every week.

02 Why it matters

Small shops usually discover a machine problem after the machine has already stopped.

0/ 10

motor failures are associated with bearings in published industry estimates. Sound alone is not enough, so the pilot adds contact vibration.

Enterprise monitoring exists, but its price and deployment model leave many breweries, workshops, and small facilities running equipment until it fails.

EarSight is a deliberately small experiment. Three Vancouver pilots will test the sensing, the weekly report, and whether a service contractor wants to offer monitoring to customers.
